## Introduction

Welcome to Shoshin ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**", or "**Company**"). Shoshin is a mobile application designed to help users build and maintain morning routines through daily checkpoints, reflections, streak tracking, group communities, and social sharing.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our app.

### Key Commitments
- **No Ads:** Shoshin does not contain advertisements or ad networks
- **No Data Sales:** We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing
- **Your Control:** You have full control over your data and can delete it anytime
- **Transparency:** This policy explains exactly what we collect and why

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: **cobwebtechnologies1@gmail.com**

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create a Shoshin account, we collect:

**Required:**
- Phone number (for OTP authentication) OR Email address (for Google OAuth login)

**Optional:**
- Full name
- Profile picture/avatar
- Location (optional)
- Bio/About section

**Why:** To authenticate your identity, enable account recovery, and provide social features.

---

### 1.2 Content You Create

All content you create is **yours and yours alone**:

- **Checkpoint Photos:** Daily photos you take as part of your routine
- **Reflections:** Written notes and journal entries about your morning
- **Group Posts:** Content shared within group communities
- **Captions & Text:** Any text you add to your posts

**Important:** You retain full ownership. We store this to enable the app to work offline, sync across devices, support group sharing, and back up your data.

---

### 1.3 Photos & Media

**What we collect:**
- Photos you capture via the app camera
- Photo metadata (timestamp, device info)
- Image data when compressed for storage

**How we store:**
- Photos stored **locally on your device first**
- Then synced to Firebase Cloud Storage
- Organized securely in our servers
- You can delete anytime

**Your control:** Delete individual photos or bulk delete via app settings.

---

### 1.4 Habit & Streak Data

We track:
- Streak count (consecutive days)
- Best streak record (all-time)
- Daily checkpoints (count)
- Consistency calendar/heatmap
- Timestamps of activities
- Habit completion status
- Group participation data

**Why:** To show your progress, calculate streaks, create heatmaps, and enable group leaderboards.

---

### 1.5 Group & Community Data

When using Groups:
- Groups you create (name, description, members)
- Groups you join (membership status, join date)
- Posts shared in groups (photos, reflections)
- Group interactions (likes, reactions, comments)
- Leaderboard rankings (streak comparison with members)
- Invitations sent/received

**Important:** Group members can only see:
- Your public group posts
- Your current streak
- Your profile (if in the group)
- They CANNOT see private reflections or photos without permission

---

### 1.6 Social Sharing Data

When you share content to Instagram, WhatsApp, Snapchat, Facebook, or Twitter:

We track (for analytics):
- Which platforms you share to
- When you share (timestamp)
- What you share (post ID, content type)
- Share success status

**Important:** We do NOT access or store your social media credentials. Social sharing uses native Android share intents.

---

### 1.7 Device & Technical Information

We automatically collect:

**Device Info:**
- Device model & manufacturer
- Android OS version
- App version number
- Unique device identifier (AAID)
- IP address
- Device language & timezone
- Screen resolution

**App Usage:**
- Screens visited
- Features used
- Time spent in app
- Errors/crashes
- Offline/online status
- Sync activity

**Why:** To improve app performance, fix bugs, monitor crashes, and understand user behavior.

---

### 1.8 Crash & Error Reports

Firebase automatically collects:
- Stack traces from crashes
- Error messages
- Device state at crash time
- Timestamps
- User ID
- App version

**Why:** To identify and fix app crashes and improve stability.

---

### 1.9 Location Data

When completing checkpoint photos:
- GPS coordinates (if you enable location)
- Rounded to 2 decimal places for privacy
- Used to verify location-based checkpoints
- **Never shared with third parties**

**Your control:** Enable/disable location in app settings anytime.

---

### 1.10 Contacts (Optional)

If you grant permission:
- Phone contacts accessed only for friend suggestions
- Phone numbers are **never stored** on our servers
- Used only for matching, then immediately discarded
- You control this permission in Android settings

## 3. How We Store Your Data

### 3.1 Local Storage (On Your Device)

**Data stored locally:**
- Your account information (cached)
- All photos (original + compressed)
- All reflections/notes
- Streak data
- Group memberships
- App preferences

**Protection:**
- Protected by device encryption
- Only accessible by Shoshin app
- You can clear anytime via Settings

**Benefits:**
- Works offline
- Fast access
- Private on your device
- You control deletion

---

### 3.2 Cloud Storage (Firebase)

**Cloud services we use:**
- Firebase Firestore (database)
- Firebase Cloud Storage (photos)
- Firebase Authentication
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)

**Where data is stored:**
- US data center (default)
- Encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
- Encrypted at rest (AES-256)
- Geographically redundant for backup

**Organization:**
- `/users/{userId}/`
- `/groups/{groupId}/`
- `/checkpoints/{userId}/`
- `/reflections/{userId}/`
- `/social_shares/{userId}/`

---

### 3.3 Data Retention

**Active Accounts:**
- We keep all data while your account is active
- Data remains as long as you use the app

**Inactive Accounts (12+ months):**
- We may delete data after 12 months of inactivity
- We'll notify you before deletion
- You can request data export before deletion

**User Deletion Request:**
- Delete all personal data within 30 days
- Retain only aggregate anonymized data (no personal identifiers)
- Cannot be reversed
- You'll receive confirmation email

**Photos:**
- Stored as long as you keep your account
- Can delete individually anytime
- Bulk delete via Settings
- Deleted copies cannot be recovered

## 6. Your Rights & Data Control

### Access Your Data
You can request a complete copy of your data by:
1. Going to Profile → Settings → Privacy & Data
2. Tap "Download My Data"
3. We'll email you a complete export within 7 days

### Delete Your Account & Data

**In-App Deletion:**
1. Open Shoshin app
2. Go to Profile → Settings
3. Scroll to "Privacy & Data"
4. Tap "Delete Account"
5. Confirm deletion
6. All data permanently deleted within 30 days

**Email Request:**
If you cannot access the app, email us at cobwebtechnologies1@gmail.com with subject "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered phone number or email address. We will process your request within 30 days.

**What Gets Deleted:**
- Your profile and personal information
- All checkpoints and streak history
- All photos and reflections
- Group memberships
- Friend connections
- All associated data

**What Cannot Be Deleted:**
- Aggregated anonymous analytics (no personal identifiers)
- Legal compliance records (kept for 7 years as required by law)

### Control Your Preferences
- **Notifications:** Enable/disable in Settings
- **Location:** Enable/disable location access
- **Contacts:** Grant/revoke contacts permission
- **Social Sharing:** Clear social sharing history anytime
